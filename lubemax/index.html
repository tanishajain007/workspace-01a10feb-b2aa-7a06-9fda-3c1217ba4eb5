    04 — Built for the real world

    Wherever performance moves, we protect it.

    From daily mobility to heavy industry, LUBEMAX protects moving systems where uptime, efficiency and reliability matter most. Eight application worlds, one standard of protection.

    • Automotive

      Engine oils, gear oils, coolants, brake fluids and maintenance products for two-wheelers, passenger cars and commercial vehicles.

    • Agriculture

      Rugged protection for tractors, pumps and agricultural equipment working through dust, heat, seasonal load and long operating hours.

    • Mining

      Heavy-duty engine, gear and hydraulic protection for high-load equipment in abrasive, contaminated and high-temperature conditions.

    • Industries

      Industrial oils that support system efficiency, component life and uptime across manufacturing and processing environments.

    • Construction

      Protection for excavators, loaders, cranes and site equipment exposed to shock loads, dust and relentless duty cycles.

    • Transport

      Long-haul and urban fleet products engineered for soot control, thermal stability, dependable pressure and vehicle availability.

    • Marine

      Application-aware protection focused on corrosion control, robust lubrication and reliability in moisture-rich marine environments.

    • Machinery

      Gear, hydraulic and general lubrication solutions for moving components, power transmission and critical machine systems.

    The right lubricant is a productivity decision.
